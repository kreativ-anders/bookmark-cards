## Short and simple.

### Free

A limited number of bookmarks, no time limit, no payment details needed.

### Premium

Unlimited bookmarks. The price is shown under (link: /#pricing text: Pricing) and at checkout, paid in advance through Stripe. No VAT is charged (§ 19 UStG). Premium renews automatically at the end of each billing period.

### Cancel anytime

Settings → *Manage Subscriptions*. Premium keeps running until the paid period ends, then your account becomes Free again.

### Delete your account

Everything is gone immediately: your bookmarks, your account and your subscription. Time already paid is not refunded. This cannot be undone.

### Changed your mind?

You can withdraw within 14 days of buying Premium. Write to us through the (link: https://kreativ-anders.de/kontakt text: contact form target: _blank) and you get your money back.

You are responsible for what you save. We keep Bookmark.cards running as well as we can, but cannot promise it is always available — export your bookmarks now and then.
