## Short and simple.

### Free

Free to use with a limited number of bookmarks.

### Premium

Unlimited bookmarks. Price and billing period are shown under (link: /#pricing text: Pricing) and at checkout. Payment runs through Stripe. No VAT is charged (§ 19 UStG). Premium renews automatically until you cancel.

### Cancel anytime

In your settings under *Manage Subscriptions*. Premium ends immediately and your account becomes Free.

### Delete your account

Your bookmarks, your account and your subscription are deleted immediately. There is no refund. This cannot be undone.

### Inactive accounts

Free accounts may be deleted, including all bookmarks, after 12 months or more of inactivity. Premium accounts are not affected.

### Changed your mind?

You can withdraw within 14 days of buying Premium. Write to us through the (link: https://kreativ-anders.de/kontakt text: contact form target: _blank) and you get your money back.

### No guarantee

We keep Bookmark.cards running as well as we can, but cannot promise it is always available. Export your bookmarks now and then. You are responsible for the links you save.
