## Your bookmarks are yours. No ads, no tracking cookies.

### What we store

Your email address, your password (encrypted) and your bookmarks. We need them to run your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Delete it yourself

In your settings you can export your data and delete your account. Deleting removes everything.

### Payments through Stripe

Your email address is shared with Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Dublin, Ireland) so Premium can be paid. Payment details go to Stripe only and never reach us. (link: https://stripe.com/privacy text: Stripe's privacy policy target: _blank) applies (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### On your device

Only cookies needed to keep you logged in. For offline use, your browser keeps a copy of your bookmarks.

### Scripts from cdnjs

When you are logged in, some scripts load from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare sees your IP address and may process it in the USA under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Visits counted by Pirsch

Pirsch Analytics counts visits and clicks without cookies and without storing your IP address. Provider: Emvi Software GmbH, Germany; data stays in Germany. More: (link: https://pirsch.io/privacy text: privacy at Pirsch target: _blank) (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Hosted by Hetzner

Hetzner Online GmbH, Gunzenhausen, Germany, runs the server and logs requests, including your IP address, to keep the site running and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
